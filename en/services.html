FAQ

Service FAQ

Common questions about booking, payment, parking and color or perm services.

Do I need to make an appointment?

ALICE mainly works by appointment to reserve enough time for each client. Same-day visits may be arranged if time is available, but advance booking is recommended.

What payment methods are available?

Cash, bank transfer, credit card, LINE Pay and JKoPay may be available. Final payment options follow in-salon notice.

Is parking nearby?

Parking is available in the W1 community underground parking lot on Zhongzheng Road, and there is also a paid parking lot across from the salon.

Can perm and color be done on the same day?

It may be possible depending on hair condition. The full process usually takes about 5 to 6 hours, and the stylist may suggest splitting services for better hair condition control.

When can I wash my hair after perming or coloring?

After a perm, wait at least 24 hours before washing. After color, wait at least 48 hours and use gentle products suitable for colored hair.
