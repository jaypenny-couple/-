About ALICE

About ALICEA 50-year salon rooted in Nankan

ALICE Hair Salon blends local trust, detailed consultation and Japanese minimal luxury aesthetics to create hairstyles that feel refined, practical and personal.

Good hair design is not only about trends. It is the balance between hair condition, scalp health, lifestyle and the stylist's professional judgment.

Brand Philosophy

Understand first, then design.

Every ALICE service begins with communication. Haircuts, color, perms and treatments are planned around the client's hair condition, daily routine and desired impression, so the style remains wearable after leaving the salon.

Local Trust

Serving Nankan and Luzhu clients for decades with stable quality and a relationship people return to.

Hair Assessment

Before color or perm services, stylists check hair texture, scalp condition and previous chemical history.

Natural Refinement

Soft lines, wearable color and a Japanese-inspired premium feel shape comfortable everyday style.

Founder Story

In 1975, ALICE began with a female founder.

ALICE Hair Salon was founded in 1975 by Ms. Lee Hsiu-Yi, whose English name Alice became the salon's brand name. From the era of traditional hair salons to today's boutique salon experience, ALICE has built local trust through professional technique, consistent service and careful client care.

1975 - The Beginning

Ms. Lee Hsiu-Yi founded ALICE Hair Salon and named the brand after her own English name, Alice.

Technical Foundation

The founder trained professionally at the Grand Hotel barbershop and served notable public figures, building a strong foundation in technique and service.

Fifty Years of Evolution

From a large traditional salon to a contemporary boutique salon, ALICE has continued to refine technique, update equipment and improve service.

Today

ALICE carries forward five decades of craft while integrating modern design, scientific hair care concepts and digital service support.
